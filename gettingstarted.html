Getting Started With Golf

Getting into golf can seem complicated at first, but you do not need to know everything before you begin. You can start by learning a few basic skills, practicing at a driving range, and becoming comfortable with the equipment. The goal when you are new to golf should be to learn the game and have fun instead of worrying about your score.

Start at the Driving Range

A driving range is one of the easiest places to start. You can practice hitting golf balls without worrying about keeping score or slowing down other golfers. Start with shorter clubs such as wedges and irons before spending too much time with the driver. Focus on making consistent contact with the ball instead of trying to hit it as far as possible.

Practice Your Short Game

Golf is more than hitting long shots. Putting and chipping are important parts of the game and are usually easier for beginners to practice. Many golf facilities have practice putting greens where you can work on controlling the speed and direction of your putts.

Consider Taking a Lesson

A golf lesson can help you learn the basic grip, stance, and swing before bad habits develop. You do not need to take lessons before playing, but even one beginner lesson can make learning the game easier. Many golf courses and driving ranges offer lessons for new golfers.

Do Not Worry About Having Everything

You do not need an expensive set of clubs or every golf accessory before you start. A basic set of clubs, golf balls, tees, and comfortable clothing is enough for your first few trips to the range. As you play more often, you can decide which equipment is worth adding.

Ready for the Next Step?

Once you are comfortable practicing, take a look at the Equipment page to learn what you actually need before playing. You can also visit the PGA of America for additional golf information and resources.
