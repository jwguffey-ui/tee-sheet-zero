Beginner Golf Equipment

When you are first getting into golf it can seem like you need a lot of equipment. The truth is that beginners only need a few basic things to get started. You can always add more equipment later as you play more and figure out what works for you.

Golf Clubs

You do not need a full set of fourteen clubs when you are learning. A basic set with a driver, a few irons, a wedge, and a putter is enough to get started. Buying used clubs can also save a lot of money when you are new to the game.

Golf Balls and Tees

There is no reason for a beginner to buy expensive golf balls. You will probably lose some while learning, so cheaper golf balls work just fine. You should also keep some tees in your bag for tee shots.

Gloves and Shoes

A golf glove can help with grip and comfort while swinging the club. Golf shoes can also help with traction, but they are not something you have to buy right away. A comfortable pair of athletic shoes will work when you are first starting.

What Do You Actually Need?

Equipment Purpose Beginner Recommendation
Golf Clubs Used to hit different golf shots. Start with a basic or used set.
Golf Balls Used throughout the round. Buy inexpensive golf balls.
Golf Glove Helps with grip and comfort. Helpful but not required.
Golf Shoes Provide traction while swinging. Optional for beginners.
Golf Tees Used to tee up the ball. Keep some in your golf bag.

Keep It Simple

You do not need expensive equipment to start playing golf. Start with the basics and add more equipment as you learn the game. Getting comfortable playing is more important than having the newest clubs or gear.
